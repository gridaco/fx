# Sena

Sena is the selected third character for FX character-content exploration,
alongside Riko and Nami. She is an adult cat-eared cafe florist with strawberry
pink hair, low twin tails, amber eyes, a cream cardigan, burgundy ribbons, a
sage apron and cocoa skirt. Her direction is cheerful and welcoming.
The cafe-florist role and personality are source design context, not narrative
constraints. FX examples may invent original roles, settings and stories while
preserving her recognizable character design.

The [local adoption manifest](sena.json) pins the full-body PNG and preserved
generation, prompt, review and reference files. They are copied into FX-owned
local storage under `www/.showcases/canonical-characters/sena/`; historical
source paths in the original records are lineage, not runtime dependencies.
`full_body.png` is the selected reference. The original review covered local
runtime use and is not evidence for a new generated performance.

The source record leaves upstream reference redistribution rights unresolved.
Keep the artwork and raw evidence out of Git and public delivery until that
issue is resolved. The upstream reference is retained only for provenance
inspection and must not enter a public example package. Selection for local
preparation does not change this status.
