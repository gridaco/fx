# Windward Meadow: expansion direction

Make a small coherent world that a reader can grasp through exactly six distinct concept images. Use only the six central subjects below; develop their relationships and material stakes rather than adding a larger cast. The supplied poster establishes a painted anime environment: huge luminous cloud masses, a broad golden meadow, blue distance and warm late-summer light. It is visual grammar, not evidence that the observatory, character or instrument appears in the poster.

- direction_exact_six: Emit exactly six entities, corresponding to Mira Vale (actor), Windward Meadow (place), the Weather Observatory (place), the Wind Crown (thing), the Meadow Cooperative (collective), and the Stilling Morning (event). Do not invent a seventh subject or force all eight ontology classes into this small census. Common grass and ordinary clouds remain environmental context, not extra entities.
- direction_cold_reader: Every entity's summary and facts must explain its role without requiring another page. Explain how the Wind Crown's rotation, the observatory's rods and the meadow's moving grass help a person tell a stuck instrument from calm weather.
- direction_material_tensions: Develop the three stated tradeoffs: protected instrument verge versus usable harvest paths; visitor access versus an unobstructed instrument lawn; and fast axle replacement versus careful restoration. Keep both sides reasonable and name labor, lost access or missing observations as the cost. No villain, disaster, violence or additional institution is needed.
- direction_observable_rules: Show ordinary physical wind, rotating cups, leaning grass, moving rods, a seed-tangled axle and deliberate maintenance. No magic, spirits, speaking machines, floating tools or miraculous weather control. The caretaker observes and repairs; she does not summon weather.
- direction_character_identity: Keep Mira visibly adult, short-haired with a warm-copper bob, round-faced and calm. Her compact silhouette has a fitted deep blue-teal utility jacket, cream shirt, slate trousers, brown work boots and a tucked rust collar accent. No cape, long skirt, loose straps, carried prop, exaggerated glamour or resemblance to an existing character.
- direction_connected_world: Use relationships across spatial, social_political, material_functional and causal_historical families to connect all six subjects. Each relationship must read as an honest sentence. Treat the Cooperative as people with shared work and authority, the Observatory as a place, and the Crown as one maintained object.
- direction_bounded_extension: Add only small original details needed to stage the existing subjects: safe access, ordinary repair gestures, lighting through shutters or the use of already named parts. Mark invented elaboration as generated_extension with source evidence and this requirement as rationale. Keep the unresolved restoration question open.
- direction_distinct_gallery: Give each subject one continuous scene with a different lesson, dominant mass, visible action and purpose. Spread intimate, human, architectural and landscape scales; include one useful interior view of the moving rods, at least four vantages and six different purposes. Prefer clear or windy daylight; a dawn or dusk view is welcome when motivated, not a requirement to darken the set.
- direction_no_explanatory_images: No maps, diagrams, charts, split panels, turnarounds, text cards, branded marks, explanatory pages or image grids. Make the contrasts legible inside the world: a stopped cup beside leaning grass, a clear path beside protected seed heads, or open shutters beside a sheltered mechanism. Tiny diegetic apron stripes may identify the Cooperative but never become a standalone emblem.
- direction_poster_grammar_only: The poster's warm grass, large cloud masses, blue hills, flower accents and painted shapes guide the medium. Do not record unseen story details as visual_observation or assume poster buildings are the named Observatory. Publication remains unauthorized and the exploration unreviewed.
