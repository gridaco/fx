# Terrain topology attribution

The 47-mask coordinate arrangement and modified 12-by-4 template are derived
from the official Godot Engine documentation terrain example. Documentation
by Juan Linietsky, Ariel Manzur, and the Godot community is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- [Documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/2d/using_tilesets.html)
- [Documentation source](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs)

The template was enlarged and annotated with a magenta exterior, cyan guide
lattice, and checker placeholder. Its pixels are preserved here because they
are the exact topology input used by the recorded showcase. This reference is
not generated artwork. The lookup and template supply geometry, not the
material appearance of the output. The topology input never becomes a runtime
asset. Source helper adaptations are covered separately by SOURCE-LICENSE.
